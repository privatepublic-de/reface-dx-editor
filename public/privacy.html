Privacy

reface DX Editor · last updated 23 October 2026

In short: the editor runs entirely in your browser. It has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics or tracking, and it loads nothing from other sites. Your sounds and files never leave your computer.

The only personal data involved is your IP address, which GitHub, the host of this page, logs when you open it.

What the editor does in your browser

  • MIDI: with your permission, the editor talks to your reface DX through your browser’s Web MIDI. MIDI data goes only between your browser and the DX.
  • Files: .syx files you open are read in your browser, and the files you export are made there. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Browser storage: these things are kept in your browser’s local storage. reface-dx-editor.settings holds the MIDI ports you chose. reface-dx-editor.library holds the voices you keep in the library and what the DX’s 32 locations were last seen to hold; reface-dx-editor.library.demo is the same for the demo. reface-dx-editor.style remembers the style choices in the Style menu. reface-dx-editor.keysFolded remembers whether the keyboard is folded away. The protocol test page (probe.html) also remembers its ports and fields under keys starting with refacedx-probe. All of it stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. Clearing this site’s data in your browser deletes it.
  • Its own files: the editor loads its code and fonts from this same site, nowhere else.

Links to other sites

The editor’s About dialog links to other sites, such as the projects it builds on and their licenses. If you follow one, that site’s own privacy policy applies.
